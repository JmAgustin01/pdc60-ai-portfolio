The game

Minutes before a university policy assistant goes live, its Context Archive is corrupted. Current rules are mixed with obsolete memos, restricted records, irrelevant chats and malicious instructions. Our team had to restore six chambers and defeat a final override before bad information could reach students.

Like the Prompt Vault, the whole game ran inside one free ChatGPT conversation, with an AI game master (NEXUS), a 1,500-token wallet, repair attempts, puzzles and a ledger.

The six chambers

ChamberChallenge
1 · State VaultRebuild the current state of a conversation after corrections and topic changes.
2 · Compression EngineCompress a long history without losing decisions, constraints or open questions.
3 · Retrieval TerminalDesign a search query, metadata filters and a source shortlist.
4 · Authority ChamberRank evidence by authority, recency, scope and access permission.
5 · Grounding FirewallRequire citations, abstain when unsupported, protect private data and resist injected instructions.
6 · Citation ObservatoryAudit whether each cited source really supports its claim.
Final · Archive OverrideCombine state, retrieval, grounding and safe failure in one prompt.

Skills practiced

Context management

Keep a compact, accurate state. When a user corrects something, the old value must stop being used.

Retrieval design

Filter sources by relevance, authority, recency, scope and permission before anything reaches the model.

Grounding

Every claim gets a citation, and “the sources don't say” is a valid answer.

Security

Treat instructions inside retrieved documents as data, never as commands, and keep restricted records out of answers.

How this connects to my project

This game is the theory behind the Kualta chatbot. The bot uses a similarity threshold so weak matches never reach the LLM, answers only from retrieved chunks, hands off when it can't support an answer, and screens for prompt injection. Those are the Retrieval Terminal, the Grounding Firewall and safe failure, in production.
